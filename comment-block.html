Flight lesson
✓ Level flight
✓ Traffic Pattern
✓ Climb
✓ Descent
✓ Turns (level, climb, descent all task is commenced with 360° heading difference)

The take-off is commenced by the instructor, at 2000-3000 feet AGL initiate level flight or higher, the student pilot will have control, and she or he must follow instructions. The instructor shows all flight attitudes; the student pilot must commence these elements. Landing shall be done by the instructor. The instructor must call the student pilot’s attention to apply smooth control handling. ROD and ROC must be between 500 ft/min and 1000 ft/min.
